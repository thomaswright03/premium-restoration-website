Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our bathroom restoration work.

We currently take on bathroom restorations only: demolition, fixtures and cabinets, tile, flooring, and painting. We don't take on kitchens, exteriors, roofing, or damage restoration (such as water, fire, or mold damage). Installing toilets, sinks, showers, or bathtubs also needs plumbing work. Plumbing and electrical work is not included in our online estimates; if your project needs any, it will add to the cost, and we'll tell you who will do it, how it will be priced, and whether your job needs any permits before any work is agreed. We do not currently hold a contractor licence.

Our labor is priced per item. For example, cabinet installation is $60 per cabinet and bathroom flooring is $5 per sq ft. Materials, permits, and any applicable taxes are separate. The chat on our home page can give you a rough, non-binding labor estimate; For a price, call us or use the Contact page; your actual price is confirmed with you after we see the job.
