What we collect

  • When you contact us. If you email or call us, including by using the form on our Get a Quote page, we receive your name, phone number, email address, the bathroom work you're interested in, and anything else you choose to tell us. The form itself does not store or send anything: it opens your own email app with a message to us, and we only receive it if you send it.
  • When we prepare a quote. If we prepare a quote for you, we record the property address and details of the job (such as room measurements and fixtures) in our internal quoting tool.
  • The chat assistant. The chat on our home page is an automated tool with scripted replies. It runs entirely in your browser. What you type into it, and any estimate or PDF it produces, is not sent to us and is not saved by us.

Cookies, analytics and third-party services

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or other tracking tools on this website. Some third-party services still receive basic technical information (such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested) when you visit:

  • Vercel hosts this website and may keep server logs. Vercel privacy policy.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) supplies the PDF tool, loaded only if you choose “Export as PDF” in the chat. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Google (Gmail) provides our email service, so emails you send us are stored by Google.

The fonts on this website are served from the same place as the website itself (Vercel), so loading a page does not send your information to a separate font provider.

Your browser's “Do Not Track” setting does not change how this website works, because we do not track visitors across websites.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare your quote.
  • If you hire us, to carry out and manage the work, and to keep the business records we are required to keep.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers listed above, or where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work (in our email, and any quote we prepared for them in our quoting tool): we keep them for about a month after our last contact with you, and then delete them. We delete them by hand, so nothing on this website deletes information automatically.
  • If you become a customer, we keep the records we need for the work and any records the law requires us to keep. We have not yet set a fixed period for customer records.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner, at any time (see below).
