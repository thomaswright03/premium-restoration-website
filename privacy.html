What we collect

  • When you contact us. If you email, call, or text us, including by using the form on our Get a Quote page, we receive your name, phone number, email address, the bathroom work you're interested in, and anything else you choose to tell us, including in any voicemail or text message you leave. The form itself does not store or send anything: it opens your own email app with a message to us, and we only receive it if you send it. When you press Send Request on the form, what you entered is sent to us through , the form service we use, which stores it and emails it to us.
  • When we prepare a quote. If we prepare a quote for you, we record the property address, your name and contact details if you have given them to us, and details of the job (such as room measurements and fixtures) in our internal quoting tool.
  • The chat assistant. The chat on our home page is an automated tool with scripted replies. It runs entirely in your browser. What you type into it, and any estimate or PDF it produces, is not sent to us and is not saved by us. So that reloading the page doesn't lose an estimate, your browser keeps your answers and the finished estimate for that browser tab only, until you close it. If you choose “Get a Quote” on a finished estimate, your browser carries a summary of the estimate to our Get a Quote form for that visit; it reaches us only if you send the form.

Cookies, analytics and third-party services

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or other tracking tools on this website. We do not use cookies, advertising pixels, or tools that follow you across websites. We do count visits and a few actions on this website, anonymously, as described below. Your browser remembers your light/dark colour theme choice on your own device; it is never sent to us. Some third-party services still receive basic technical information (such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested) when you visit:

  • Vercel hosts this website and may keep server logs. Vercel privacy policy.
  • receives and stores what you send with the form on our Get a Quote page, and emails it to us. Its privacy policy
  • counts visits to this website and a few actions on it: an estimate being started or finished (or its PDF failing), the Get a Quote page being opened from an estimate, and a quote request being sent, not sent, or opened in your email app. It receives basic technical information about each visit (such as the page, your browser and device type, and your approximate location from your IP address) and shows us only totals. It does not use cookies, and nothing you type into the chat or the form is included. It is also told when something on this website goes wrong for you, so we can fix it: a script error or a file that fails to load (which file and line, the kind of error and the page), or the website's settings not loading. The error's own message is never sent, so nothing you typed can be included. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is counted or reported. Its privacy policy
  • Google (Gmail) provides our email service, so emails you send us are stored by Google.
  • Our phone service provider carries calls and texts to (385) 356-8733, so it handles your phone number and any voicemail or text message you leave.

The fonts and the PDF tool on this website are served from the same place as the website itself (Vercel), so loading a page or exporting an estimate as a PDF does not send your information to a separate provider.

Your browser's “Do Not Track” setting does not change how this website works, because we do not track visitors across websites.

If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal, this website does not count your visit or anything you do on it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only:

We will tell you who they are before any work is agreed; and
  • where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work (in our email, any voicemail or text messages, requests held by our form service, and any quote we prepared for them in our quoting tool): we keep them for about a month after our last contact with you, and then delete them. We delete them by hand, so nothing on this website deletes information automatically.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner, at any time (see below).
