Bathroom Restoration Services

02

03

Tile, Flooring & Paint

Wall and floor tile, bathroom flooring, and painting of walls and ceiling.

Good to know before you ask for a price

  • We currently take on bathroom restorations only.
  • We do not currently hold a contractor licence.
  • Plumbing and electrical work is not included in our online estimates. Toilets, sinks, showers, and bathtubs also need plumbing work, which costs extra. If your project needs any plumbing or electrical work, it will add to the cost, and we'll tell you who will do it and how it will be priced before any work is agreed.
